✅ Biometric Aadhaar Authentication Made Easy — Complete Self e-KYC

  1. Visit your nearest Indane distributor or authorised e-KYC centre.
  2. Carry your Aadhaar card, consumer number, and registered mobile number.
  3. Place your finger on the biometric (fingerprint) device for Aadhaar authentication.
  4. Alternatively, complete self e-KYC via the Indane / IndianOil One portal or app — scan QR, log in with mobile OTP, verify Aadhaar details.
  5. e-KYC can also be done through Common Service Centres (CSC/Jan Seva Kendra).
  6. e-KYC is mandatory to continue receiving LPG subsidy benefits — do it today!
